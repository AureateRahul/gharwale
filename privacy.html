Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This policy explains what the Gharwale app (for Mac, Windows and Linux) and this website do with your information. In short, Gharwale runs on your computer, keeps your information on your computer, and doesn't sell or share it.

What Gharwale stores on your computer

  • Your settings: the nickname you choose, language, tone, reminder times, quiet hours and similar choices.
  • Reminder history: how many check-ins you had today and when each reminder last appeared, so it doesn't repeat itself.
  • Your Pro license key, if you buy Pro.

This stays in the app's folder on your computer. We don't have servers that collect it, and there are no ads or tracking in the app.

Google Calendar

On Windows and Linux, you can choose to connect Google Calendar with "Sign in with Google" so Maa or Papa can remind you a few minutes before a meeting. This is optional and off until you connect it.

  • What we ask for: read-only access to the events in your main Google Calendar, plus your email address so the app can show which account is connected.
  • What the app reads: each upcoming event's title, start time, whether it was cancelled or declined, and any Google Meet, Zoom, Teams or Webex link, so it can show a "Join" button.
  • What the app never does: it can't create, change or delete events, and it doesn't read your email, contacts or other Google data.
  • Where it goes: event details are fetched directly from Google to your computer and kept only in the app's memory while it runs. They are not saved to disk, and not sent to us or anyone else.
  • Your sign-in token is stored on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's protection (Windows Data Protection or the Linux keyring).
  • Disconnecting: click Disconnect in Gharwale's Settings. This deletes the token from your computer and tells Google to cancel the access. You can also remove access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Gharwale's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't use Google data for advertising, don't sell it, and don't use it to train AI models.

Mac Calendar

On Mac, Gharwale reads the calendars already in Apple's Calendar app, only after you allow it in macOS. The same rules apply: read-only, only titles, times and meeting links, and nothing leaves your Mac.

Payments

If you buy Pro, the payment is handled by our payment provider. We receive your license key and purchase confirmation, not your card details.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies or analytics. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address like any website you visit.

Children

Gharwale isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
